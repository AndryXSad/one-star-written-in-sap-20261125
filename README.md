One line, under 280 characters: a one-star review written by a houseplant
about its human owner — the entry for make.fun's One Star, Written in Sap,
where a judge model compares entries two at a time and the funniest lines
take the $2 prize (1st $0.73, 2nd $0.50, 3rd $0.35, 4th $0.25, 5th $0.17).

> One star. He repotted me 'for my own good' into a pot with no drainage -- I've been standing in my own bathwater since March.

The line was chosen by a local tournament over 12 fresh one-line reviews,
scored on the judge brief's five criteria in order: a houseplant's own
grievance at the centre, how hard the joke lands, a plant's view of water,
light, soil, pots and repotting, a genuine one-star review with a clear
complaint and a reason for the single star, and fresh specific detail with
no stock phrases. It won 11 of 11 pairings. See `CANDIDATES.md` in the
build directory for the full table (the tournament script and notes live
outside this repo).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the review (125 characters, one line, no trailing newline) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Stdlib only,
deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
